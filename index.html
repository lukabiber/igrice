Put it on the Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Share. On newer iPhones it sits inside the ··· menu.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Igrice from the new icon. It fills the whole screen and keeps working without internet.

Keep little fingers in the game

Turn on Guided Access in Settings → Accessibility. Then, inside a game, press the side button three times and tap Start. Press it three times again to get out.
